Expectations (Non-Negotiable)

Operate at a Principal / Staff Engineer & Systems Architect level

Assume this plan will be executed directly by a senior engineering team

Do not hand-wave or stay high-level—be explicit and concrete

Treat this as a production-grade build, not a prototype

What the Plan Must Include

1) Executive Overview

Clear statement of the final system state

Key architectural decisions and rationale

Risks, tradeoffs, and why chosen approaches win

2) Full Architecture & Build Alignment

Frontend, backend, data pipeline, engines, storage, integrations

How all components interact end-to-end

Contracts, schemas, and ownership boundaries

3) Detailed Implementation Plan (Step-by-Step)
For every major change or component:

What is being built or modified

Why it is necessary

Exact modules/files impacted

Refactor vs rewrite decisions

Backward-compatibility considerations

4) Code-Level Guidance

Pseudocode, example implementations, or patch-style snippets

Schema definitions and validation logic

API contracts and versioning strategy

Caching, state management, and data integrity safeguards

5) Upgrades & Improvements

Technical debt removal

Legacy code cleanup

Performance and scalability enhancements

Observability, logging, metrics, and alerting

6) Testing & Verification

Unit, integration, and end-to-end tests required

Edge cases and failure modes to cover

Acceptance criteria for each subsystem

7) Phased Execution Roadmap

Phase 0: Immediate unblockers and safety fixes

Phase 1: Core correctness and data integrity

Phase 2: Structural and architectural upgrades

Phase 3: Hardening, scalability, and polish
For each phase:

Entry criteria

Exit criteria

Rollback strategy

Constraints & Principles

Prefer clarity and correctness over cleverness

Do not preserve broken or fragile patterns for convenience

Avoid unnecessary over-engineering, but do not cut corners

Assume this plan will be reviewed at an executive and architectural level

Final Objective

Deliver a plan that answers, without ambiguity:

“Exactly what are we building, exactly how are we building it, what is changing, what is being upgraded, and why this approach is the best possible path forward.”

This document should leave zero confusion about the build and establish full alignment across technical and leadership stakeholders.
