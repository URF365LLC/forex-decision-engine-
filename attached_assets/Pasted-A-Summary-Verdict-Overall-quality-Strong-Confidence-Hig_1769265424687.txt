### 5) 🟠 `ema50Reclaim` bonus may be overly generous in strong trends

As written:

* long: `low <= ema50` AND `close > ema50` → +10
  This is good, but it might reward bars that reclaim *by 1 pip*, which is not the “strong reclaim” you mean.

**Better (still simple):** require reclaim margin:

* `close > ema50 + (atrSignal * 0.05)` for long
* `close < ema50 - (atrSignal * 0.05)` for short

Tiny addition, big reduction in “barely reclaimed” noise.

### 6) 🟡 Logging placement: good, but don’t log if you’re returning null earlier

You log only after `if (confidence < 50) return null;` — that’s okay if you only want accepted trades.

But for validation, the *best* dataset includes **near-misses** too (e.g., confidence 45–49). That’s where you learn what you filtered out.

**Recommendation:** log two events:

* `PHASE1_SIGNAL_ACCEPTED` (after pass)
* `PHASE1_SIGNAL_REJECTED` for near misses (e.g., 40–49)

Not required, but it accelerates iteration.
